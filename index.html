Run a small Pakistani restaurant. Haul ingredients from the storeroom, cook, keep the fridge stocked, and make the daily quota.

WASDWalk (Shift to run)MouseLook (click the game first)ETake from shelves, use stations, serve guestsQDrop the last thing you're holding on the floor (E picks it up)FHold to charge, let go to throw (throw food straight to the guest who ordered it)VFirst / third person1–9Pick an option in menus (or click)

Storeroom shelves → carry raw ingredients to the kitchen (or leave them on the prep counter) → cook → serve. Cans and ayran come in crates: carry them to the drinks fridge to restock it. Order more stock on the backroom PC; it arrives by tuk tuk.

Every evening the day's bills (the quota) come out of your cash. Can't cover it and the rest goes on a loan at 10% a day. Owe more than $500 and the bank closes you down.
